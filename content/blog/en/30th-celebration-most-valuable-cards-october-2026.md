## The RGB Mew: red, green, blue

Pokémon publishes no odds for them, and the numbers going around are only estimates from the community, anywhere between roughly 1 in 3,000 and 1 in 20,000 packs. That is why they cost what they cost.

The red **Mew R** leads at **$6,000.27** (cheapest listing $4,989.99). The blue **Mew B** follows at **$5,322.74**, and the green **Mew G** at **$3,738.75**. The market is thin, with very few copies listed, so these prices can jump around a lot from one day to the next. Counterfeits exist for this kind of card: buy graded copies or from sellers you trust.

On Cardmarket, the three also have a price in euros: **€2,777.22** (R), **€2,653.27** (G) and **€2,517.06** (B). That guide mixes every language, and it's lower than the US market price, so the two don't line up.

## What about the 30 classic reprints?

The set also holds 30 classic cards from all over the game's history. Most are cheap, but a few stand out: **Lugia** at **$180.13**, **Charizard** at **$141.93** and **Magikarp** at **$87.56**. Eleven of the thirty are above $20.

At the other end, the **29 Pikachu Rare** priced so far cost between about $0.59 and $2.06 each: the 30 Pikachu hunt is cheap, which is exactly why it's fun.

## How much does a complete set cost?

Adding up one of each regular card of the English list (68 commons, 18 rares, 12 double rares, 18 illustration rares, 10 special illustration rares, 2 Futuristic Rares and the Pikachu Rares) gives about **$696**. Special illustration rares alone are about **$453** of that, and the two Futuristic Rares **$103**. Add the 30 classic reprints for roughly **$782** more. The three RGB Mew would add **$15,062** on their own.

## FAQ

### What is the most expensive card in 30th Celebration?

The red **Mew R (RGB Rare)**, at **$6,000.27** TCGplayer market price on October 12, 2026, ahead of the blue (**$5,322.74**) and green (**$3,738.75**) versions.

### What is the most valuable card without the RGB Mew?

The special illustration rare **Mew ex (152)**, at **$86.48** on October 12, 2026. Among the classic reprints, **Lugia** is higher at **$180.13**.

### How much does it cost to complete 30th Celebration?

About **$696** for one of each regular card (RGB Mew and classic reprints excluded), or roughly **$1,478** with the 30 classic reprints, TCGplayer market prices of October 12, 2026.
