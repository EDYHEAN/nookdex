Ever seen the same card at $600 in one shop and about half that in another? Often it isn't a scam, just a different language. We take [Ascended Heroes](/en/sets/ascended-heroes) (ASC, Mega Evolution series) and look at what changes between the English, French and Japanese prints.

Some landmarks: the English and French sets both have 295 cards. The Japanese counterpart, *MEGA Dream ex* (M2a), came out earlier, on November 28, 2025, with its own numbering and 250 cards in our data. Numbers don't match across languages, so compare the Pokémon and the rarity, not the number.

## Three markets, three ways to price

- **English**: TCGplayer market price, in dollars, for English cards.
- **French**: Cardmarket in euros. Cardmarket's price guide mixes every language of a card, so the French figure also reflects other languages' sales.
- **Japanese**: Cardmarket in euros, but Japanese prints are products of their own there, so it's the Japanese card's price.

NookDex follows the same rule: each card shows the price of its own language, and your totals are converted into the currency you pick.

## Why such a gap?

Prices come from demand, not a price list. Japanese prints draw fewer buyers outside Japan, so the same artwork sells for less. And watch the distance between trend and cheapest listing: on French Mega Dragonite ex the trend (€407.41) sits far above the lowest offer (€160), the sign of a price that moves fast. Check before you buy.

## So which print?

- **You play or collect for fun**: pick the language you read. Japanese is a great way to get the same art for less.
- **You're completing a binder**: stay in one language.
- **You plan to resell**: pick the market you'll sell on, TCGplayer for English, Cardmarket for French.
- **You buy abroad**: check condition and the listing's language before paying.

## FAQ

### Are Japanese Pokémon cards cheaper than English ones?

Often: on November 4, 2026, Japanese Mega Gengar ex (Special Illustration Rare) is €278.17 on Cardmarket, against $677.21 for the English print on TCGplayer.

### Can I compare an English and a French price directly?

Not directly. English prices come from TCGplayer in dollars for the US market; French prices come from Cardmarket in euros with every language mixed.

### Do card numbers match between languages?

No. Ascended Heroes has 295 cards in English and French, its Japanese counterpart M2a has 250 in our data, with different numbering. Match by Pokémon and rarity.
