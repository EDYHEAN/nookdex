[Pitch Black](/en/sets/pitch-black) came out in July, and the next big Mega Evolution set is already around the corner. Here is what's **confirmed** so far about **Delta Reign**. Its cards aren't in NookDex's data yet, so no prices today; we'll come back after launch.

## Date, size and prerelease

Delta Reign comes out on **November 6, 2026**, everywhere on the same day. Pokémon announces **over 135 cards**, including over 20 Trainer cards and over 35 special illustrations. These are minimums, so the final list can still move.

Prerelease tournaments and Build & Battle Boxes start **October 24, 2026** at Play! Pokémon stores, two weeks before release.

One caveat: some sites say 113 cards, but that figure comes from the Japanese original's list (Storm Emeralda, M6). For the English set, stick with the official numbers above.

## Mega Rayquaza ex leads the way

This is THE card everyone is waiting for. **Mega Rayquaza ex** returns to the TCG after XY – Ancient Origins. Early lists point to a special illustration rare and a gold Mega Hyper Rare version, like recent sets.

No price exists yet, and pre-release guesses deserve some suspicion: prices swing a lot in the first days.

## Split Stadiums

The real novelty. Six Stadium cards make up three Legendary Stadiums: each Stadium is cut into two cards sharing one illustration. You need both halves in hand to play them, and once in play they count as a single Stadium.

It echoes the LEGEND cards of the HeartGold & SoulSilver era. For collectors, it means you'll need both halves to complete the set.

## What will be on the shelves?

The usual products are expected: booster packs, a 36-pack booster box, a bundle, an Elite Trainer Box and a Build & Battle Box. Exact contents and prices weren't officially detailed when I wrote this, so I'm not quoting prices.

## Worth waiting for?

If you chase Mega ex cards or Rayquaza, yes, especially the special illustrations. If you just want a complete set, give it a few weeks: launch prices usually settle after the first days.
