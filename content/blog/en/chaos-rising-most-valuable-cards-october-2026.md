[Chaos Rising](/en/sets/chaos-rising) is the fourth big set of the Mega Evolution era, and Mega Greninja ex steals the show. Here is what the cards are worth, based on the **TCGplayer market price of October 8, 2026** (English cards, US dollars).

::card[en:me04-116]

## Mega Greninja ex, twice on the podium

No surprise: it's THE card of the set. **Mega Greninja ex (116)**, the special illustration rare, sits at **$132.60**, and the cheapest listing is $127.

Right behind, **Mega Greninja ex (122)**, the golden Mega Hyper Rare, is at **$125.58**. Both are sliding a little: on Cardmarket, the 116 averages €138.65 over 7 days and €154.76 over 30 days, the 122 €121.93 and €136.17 (those averages are in euros and mix every language). Five months after release, the market is calming down.

## What about Japanese?

The Japanese counterpart, *Ninja Spinner* (M4), came out on March 13, 2026, and its cards are separate products on Cardmarket. Prices there are in euros: the **Japanese Mega Greninja ex Mega Hyper Rare (M4-120)** has a Cardmarket trend of **€349.20**, and the special illustration rare (M4-114) **€135.28**. The Japanese Mega Hyper Rare is worth well over twice the English one in raw numbers.

If you collect in English, your binder doesn't change, but it explains why sellers hold firm on Mega Hyper Rares.

## How much does the full set cost?

Adding the market price of all 122 cards (one of each, no reverses) gives **$475.86**. The 6 special illustration rares are **$260.60** of that and the Mega Hyper Rare **$125.58**: together about 81% of the total.

The rest is cheap: the 38 commons and 26 uncommons add up to **$7.85**. Without the top secrets, finishing the set is a weekend project.
