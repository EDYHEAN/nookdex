## Mega Evolution: the current star

The Mega Hyper Rare (#188) leads the recent cards: $181.82 market price, cheapest listing at $155.58. The set came out on September 26, 2025.

The Special Illustration Rare (#179) is cheaper at $158.27, with a low of $147.40. The two are closer than their rarities suggest.

## The old ones are not cheap

The **Lucario LV.X** of Mysterious Treasures (2007) is at $216.04, with its cheapest listing at $222.53: you pay for it, there is no discount hunting here.

The Furious Fists (2014) pair is strong too: **M Lucario EX** Secret Rare at $147.33 and **Lucario EX** at $139.83.

## Sun & Moon GX cards
