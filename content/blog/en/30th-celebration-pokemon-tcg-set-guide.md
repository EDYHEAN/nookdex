For the game's 30th birthday, Pokémon put out a set that feels more like a party than a regular expansion. Foil everywhere, Pikachu to collect, and a nod to the cards that made history. Here's what we know, what the card list shows, and what we'd chase first.

::card[en:30th-157]

## What's in the set?

The set has **128 numbered cards** ("official" count) and goes up to **158 with the secret rares** in the data we follow. Each pack holds five foil cards plus a foil basic Energy, according to retailer listings.

There are also **30 classic cards** coming back from across the game's history, from Base Set Charizard to more recent hits. TCGdex doesn't list them yet, so no card page or price here: we'll add them once they're available.

## The rarities, card by card

What the English list holds (classic reprints aside):

|---|---|
| Common | 68 |

| Special illustration rare | 10 |
| Futuristic Rare | 2 |

The **30 Pikachu Rares** are the running theme: each pack hides one, and getting them all is a fun collecting goal. At the very top, the **Futuristic Rare** is the set's brand-new rarity: just two cards, **Mewtwo ex (157)** and **Mew ex (158)**, in YOSHIROTTEN's graphic style. The set's Energy cards share that look.

## Which cards to chase?

No surprise: it's those two Futuristic Rares everyone wants. Right behind come the **ten special illustration rares** (147 to 156): Fuecoco ex, Greninja ex, two Pikachu ex, Mewtwo ex, Mew ex, Sylveon ex, Gengar ex, Jirachi ex and Salamence ex.

## Is it worth opening?

It's all foil and every pack has its Pikachu, so the opening is fun even without a big hit. As a collection, it's a celebration set rather than a competitive one. On the product side, a six-pack Booster Bundle and a nine-pack Elite Trainer Box are listed by retailers.

As for prices, hang tight: there's no TCGplayer market price for these cards in our data yet. We'll update as soon as the numbers land.
