Cardmarket is where most European collectors buy single cards. It's cheap and it works well, as long as you know how to read a listing. Here are the habits to take, based on [Cardmarket's own help](https://help.cardmarket.com/new-user-guide).

## Who's selling? Private, professional, Powerseller

A **private seller** has a small collection and often lower prices. A **professional** is a shop: buying from them feels like any online store. A **Powerseller** is a professional Cardmarket selected for a strong reputation and high sales volume.

None is "bad" by nature. For an expensive card, many collectors prefer a seller with lots of reviews. The filters on the left of the offers list let you narrow by language and seller type.

## Read the ratings

Buyers rate sellers after each shipment, and those ratings are the seller's reputation. According to Cardmarket, the usual rating is "Very Good": anything lower typically points to an unresolved dispute or a real mistake (wrong language, condition or card). Read the bad ratings before you look at the price.

## Paying and being protected

Your cart asks for no payment details: you "commit to buy", then pay by bank transfer, PayPal or card. You have **seven days** to pay, or the seller can cancel without your consent.

The **Trustee service** holds your payment until you confirm arrival. It applies to orders **over €25** and to a new seller's first five sales, for a small extra fee. It also covers shipping damage the post office doesn't, and ends once you confirm delivery.

## When the parcel arrives

Open it, compare with the listing, and only then confirm. If a tracked order has a problem, set it to "Not received - Issue" **before** confirming. If the card isn't what the listing said, write to the seller first: it's often settled with a refund or a reshipment.

## The habits in five lines

- Filter language and condition before you look at the price (see [our condition guide](/blog/card-condition-guide-pokemon-cardmarket)).
- Be wary of a price far under the trend: check the seller and ask for front and back photos.
- Stay inside Cardmarket's payment, never a direct transfer outside the platform.
- Remember Trustee for orders over €25.
- Confirm arrival only when everything is right.

## FAQ

### What's the difference between private, professional and Powerseller on Cardmarket?

A private seller has a small collection, a professional is a shop, and a Powerseller is a professional Cardmarket picked for its reputation and sales volume.

### How does Cardmarket protect buyers?

With the Trustee service, Cardmarket holds your payment until you confirm arrival. It applies to orders over €25 and to a new seller's first five sales.

### How long do I have to pay a Cardmarket order?

Seven days after ordering, otherwise the seller can cancel without your consent. Three or more missed payments suspend your ability to buy.
