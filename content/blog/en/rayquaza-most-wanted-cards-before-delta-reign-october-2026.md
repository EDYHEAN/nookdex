## Evolving Skies, Rayquaza's home

Released on August 27, 2021, [Evolving Skies](/en/sets/evolving-skies) made Rayquaza its star. **Rayquaza VMAX 218** sits at **$1,268.52**, and the cheapest listing is $989.01, so the gap to the market price is much smaller than it looks on the French side.

The **Rayquaza V 194** is at $480.92. Its Cardmarket 7-day sales average (€600.97) is above the 30-day one (€425.63): it's climbing.

Want the same Pokémon for less? The other VMAX of the set, **Rayquaza VMAX 217**, is **$108.38**.
