> **In short**
>
> - One copy of every card in **Pitch Black** (ME05, 120 cards) is **$483.96** at TCGplayer market prices on November 19, 2026.
> - **99 of the 120 cards** cost $2 or less: **$37.26** together. The **7 priciest cards** make up **$387**, 80% of the bill.
> - Smart order: finish the "core" set first, and leave the special illustration rares for last (or never).

Finishing a set is the best kind of checkbox. But the bill depends a lot on how you go about it. Let's use [Pitch Black](/en/sets/pitch-black), the Mega Evolution set released on July 17, 2026, as a test case. All prices are TCGplayer market prices for English cards on November 19, 2026.

::card[en:me05-116]

## Where the money really goes

No surprise: a handful of cards carry the bill. The Special Illustration Rare Mega Darkrai ex (116) is **$127.73**, and its Mega Hyper Rare version (120) **$98.00**. Together, **$225.73**, almost half the set.

Add Morpeko ex (117, $57.44), Mega Zeraora ex (114, $32.38), Mega Chandelure ex (115, $28.09) and Gwynn (119, $24.20), and seven cards cost **$387.08**. The other 113 cards add up to **$96.88**.

::card[en:me05-117]

## The "core" set is surprisingly cheap

Look at the other end of the list: 80 cards are $0.50 or less, for **$12.34 in total**. And 99 cards are $2 or less: **$37.26** for about 83% of the set.

| Tier | Cards | Running cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| $0.50 or less | 80 | $12.34 |
| $2 or less | 99 | $37.26 |
| Whole set | 120 | $483.96 |

The catch: at these prices, shipping is the real cost. A 10-cent card ships for the same price as a $20 one, so buy from one seller at a time.

::card[en:me05-001]

## Three ways to do it

1. **Pack by pack.** The most fun, the least predictable: you'll pile up duplicate commons and still miss the rares.
2. **A bulk lot of commons and uncommons.** Sellers sell lots of the cheapest rarities, and that sorts out most of the set in one order.
3. **Rares one by one.** For the double rares, ultra rares and illustration rares, buy singles and check the [card's condition](/blog/card-condition-guide-pokemon-cardmarket).

Suggested order: the bulk first, then the mid rares, and only at the end the six special illustration rares and the Mega Hyper Rare. From there it's a collector's call: the set "without the 7 priciest" costs about $97.

Looking for packs? Search [Pokemon TCG Pitch Black on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Pokemon+TCG+Pitch+Black), and keep singles for TCGplayer.
