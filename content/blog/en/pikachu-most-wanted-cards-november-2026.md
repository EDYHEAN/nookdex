## The modern queen: Ascended Heroes

This is THE Pikachu card right now: the Pikachu ex 276/295 from Ascended Heroes, released January 30, 2026. Market price $529.91, cheapest listing $480.15, and a 30-day Cardmarket average (in euros) of €880.54, so it has cooled a bit.

## Surging Sparks holds its ground

The Pikachu ex 238/252 from Surging Sparks (November 2024) is at $225.81 with a cheapest listing of $190. A year on, it's still among the top Pikachu cards.

## The older cards that refuse to fade

Vintage and mid-era cards stay expensive. Cosmic Eclipse's Secret Rare Pikachu (2019) is at $214.36 on November 9. Legend Maker's **Pikachu δ** (2006) shows $287.54, but its cheapest listing is $30.95: a rare card whose condition makes all the difference.
