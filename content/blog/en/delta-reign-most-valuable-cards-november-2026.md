## Mega Rayquaza ex, twice at the top

No surprise: this is THE card everyone wants. The special illustration rare **Mega Rayquaza ex (138)** sits at **$311.28**, with the cheapest listing around **$300**.

Right behind it, the golden Mega Hyper Rare **Mega Rayquaza ex (141)** is at **$260.91** (cheapest listing $209.01). That gap between market price and cheapest listing is worth a look before you buy.

## Where the value is for the price

Illustration rares make great cards without the big-money tag: **Eevee (114)** is $29.32 and **Groudon (111)** $22.67. **Raikou ex (136)** at $88.86 is the entry ticket if Mega Rayquaza is out of budget.

## What does the full set cost?

Adding up the market price of all 141 cards (one version of each, no reverses) gives **$1,000.53**. The top 7 cards account for $774.09, roughly 77%. The 83 commons and uncommons together come to just **$8.98**.

So a set "without the top chase cards" is very affordable. The first seven rows of the table make all the difference.
