You open a pack, you see a shiny card, and you wonder if it's a "real rare". Here's how to read rarities, with examples from [Pitch Black](/en/sets/pitch-black), out July 17, 2026. Prices are the **TCGplayer market price of October 19, 2026** (English cards, US market).

## The basics: common, uncommon, rare

This is most of every pack. The symbol at the bottom of the card tells you: a **circle** for a common, a **diamond** for an uncommon, a **star** for a rare.

Pitch Black has 37 commons, 26 uncommons and 11 rares. The priciest common is $0.23 (Wailmer), the priciest uncommon $0.19 (Slowbro), the priciest rare $0.23 (Voltaic Lightning Energy). Of those 11 rares, 9 are Pokémon and 2 are special Energy cards, and the Energy tops the list.

## Reverse holos: the same card, shinier

A reverse holo is the same card with a holo background, and it has its own price. In the French file the common Tropius is €0.02 normal and €0.06 reverse (Cardmarket, same day). Not a big deal per card, but a reverse master set costs more than a normal one.

## Double Rare: the ex cards

**Double Rare** cards are the Pokémon ex, like Mega Chandelure ex (38) here. Pitch Black has 10 of them, between $0.47 and $0.77. They're strong in play, not pricey to collect.

## Secret cards: where prices take off

After the 84 main cards come the 36 secret ones, numbered past the set total:

- **Illustration Rare** (11 cards): a Pokémon in a painted scene, $1.12 to $6.34 (Slowbro).
- **Ultra Rare** (18 cards): full-art Pokémon and Trainers, $1.17 to $10.11 (Misty's Vitality).
- **Special Illustration Rare** (6 cards): the same idea, with a scene that spills over the whole card. $23.05 to $136.89.
- **Mega Hyper Rare** (1 card): the golden one, Mega Darkrai ex (120), at $135.53.

## What it means for you

Two simple rules. Don't judge a card by its shine: an Ultra Rare can cost less than an Illustration Rare, because the Pokémon matters. And check the condition: a damaged card loses value fast.

## FAQ

### What's the difference between Illustration Rare and Special Illustration Rare?

Both are secret cards with painted art. In Pitch Black the Special Illustration Rare costs far more: $23.05 to $136.89, against $1.12 to $6.34 for the Illustration Rare (TCGplayer market price, October 19, 2026).

### How many secret cards are in Pitch Black?

36: 11 Illustration Rares, 18 Ultra Rares, 6 Special Illustration Rares and 1 Mega Hyper Rare, on top of the 84 main cards.

### Is a rarer card always pricier?

No. In Pitch Black the best Ultra Rare ($10.11) beats most Illustration Rares, and the Mega Hyper Rare ($135.53) sits just below the top Special Illustration Rare ($136.89).
