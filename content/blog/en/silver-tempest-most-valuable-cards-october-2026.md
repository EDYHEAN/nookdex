Silver Tempest is the last big set of the Sword & Shield era, out on November 11, 2022. On paper: 195 official cards, numbered up to 215 with the secret rares, plus a 30-card Trainer Gallery (TG01 to TG30). That's 245 cards to file, and as usual a handful of them blow up the bill. We looked at the **TCGplayer market prices** NookDex recorded on October 2, 2026.

::card[en:swsh12-186]

## The alternate art Lugia V, still alone at the top

No surprise there: the **Lugia V (186/195)** alternate art is THE card everyone wants. Market price **$517.78**, cheapest listing $429.99.

Over on Cardmarket, where every language of the card is sold together, it's clearly cooling off: **€381.38** on average over the last 7 days, against **€550.88** over 30 days. If it's still missing from your binder, keep an eye on it.

## Rising and falling

On the way up: **Alolan Vulpix VSTAR (197)** sits at $24.41, way above its Cardmarket averages (€14.34 over 7 days, €14.17 over 30). The Trainer Gallery **Blaziken V (TG14)** is climbing on Cardmarket too, from a €26.68 monthly average to a €43.66 trend.

On the way down: the Lugia, as we saw, and the Trainer Gallery **Rayquaza VMAX (TG20)**, whose 30-day Cardmarket average (€170.08) is above where it is today.

## How much is the complete set?

Add up the market prices of all 245 cards and you land around **$1,426** for a complete set, one copy of each. The alternate art Lugia alone is more than a third of that. Commons and uncommons go for cents: it all comes down to about ten cards.
