Released on November 11, 2022, **Silver Tempest** (SWSH12) closes the Sword & Shield era. The set has 195 official cards, numbered up to 215 with the secret rares, plus a 30-card Trainer Gallery (TG01 to TG30): 245 cards in all. Here are the ones worth the most today, from the **TCGplayer market prices** NookDex recorded on October 2, 2026.

::card[en:swsh12-186]

## The alternate art Lugia V, far ahead

No surprise: the **Lugia V (186/195)** alternate art is still the set's chase card, with a market price of **$517.78** and the cheapest listing at $429.99. On Cardmarket, where every language of the card is sold together, it is clearly going down: its sales average **€381.38** over the last 7 days against **€550.88** over 30 days.

## Rising and falling

- **Rising**: Alolan Vulpix VSTAR (197) is at $24.41, well above its Cardmarket averages (€14.34 over 7 days, €14.17 over 30 days). The Trainer Gallery Blaziken V (TG14) also climbs on Cardmarket, from a €26.68 30-day average to a €43.66 trend.
- **Falling**: the alternate art Lugia V, as seen above, and the Trainer Gallery Rayquaza VMAX (TG20), whose 30-day Cardmarket average of €170.08 is above today's level.

::card[en:swsh12-197]

## The whole set

Added up, the market prices of all 245 cards come to about **$1,426** for a complete set with one copy of each card, more than a third of it for the alternate art Lugia V alone. Commons and uncommons are worth cents: most of the bill comes down to about ten cards.
