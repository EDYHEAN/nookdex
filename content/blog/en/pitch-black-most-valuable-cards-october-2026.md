[Pitch Black](/en/sets/pitch-black) is the fifth big set of the Mega Evolution era, and the smallest of the series at 120 cards (84 + 36 secret). It's built on the Mega Dimension DLC of Pokémon Legends: Z-A, with Mega Darkrai ex and Mega Zeraora ex on the front line. Here's what it costs, using the **TCGplayer market price** (English cards, US market) from October 4, 2026.

## Mega Darkrai ex, twice at the top

No surprise: it's THE card everyone wants. The special illustration rare **Mega Darkrai ex (116)** sits at **$163.52**, with the cheapest listing at $160.

Right behind it, the golden **Mega Darkrai ex (120)** Mega Hyper Rare is at **$146.88**. The cheapest listing of this one is $139.

## What does a complete set cost?

Adding up the market price of all 120 cards (one version of each, no reverses) gives **$636.03**. The top seven cards make about 82% of that, so a set "without the chase cards" is very affordable: the 63 commons and uncommons add up to $6.80.

Prices of the same cards in Europe differ: on Cardmarket, which mixes every language, the Mega Darkrai ex (116) sits at €193.83 on the same day, and its 30-day average was €237.71, so it has been sliding for a while.
