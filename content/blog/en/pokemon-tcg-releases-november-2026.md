November is a one-big-date month. Here's what's **confirmed**, what isn't, and what came out last month. The full list lives on our [release calendar](/en/release-calendar).

## Delta Reign, November 6

**Mega Evolution: Delta Reign** (ME06) lands on **November 6, 2026**. Pokémon says over 135 cards, with more than 20 Trainers and more than 35 special illustrations. The featured Megas are Mega Rayquaza ex, Mega Golurk ex, Mega Malamar ex and Mega Golisopod ex.

Prerelease events started October 24 at Play! Pokémon stores, with Build & Battle Boxes. Our [Delta Reign guide](/blog/delta-reign-set-guide-before-release-me06) has the details. Its cards aren't in NookDex yet: first prices come after launch.

## What else?

Not much that's official. Some retailer calendars list a Japanese set for November 27, but The Pokémon Company hasn't announced it, so no name and no date from me: it's a rumor.

The next dated product I found is the **Unova Heavy Hitters Premium Collection**, announced in the US for **December 4, 2026** at $59.99 (four promos, six Black Bolt and six White Flare packs, a TCG Live code).

## What came out last month

No new set in October. The latest international release is [30th Celebration](/en/sets/30th-celebration), on September 16. In Japan, the nine regional card sets came out October 16.
